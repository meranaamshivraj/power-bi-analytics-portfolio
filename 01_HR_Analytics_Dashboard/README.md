# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data to understand workforce distribution, employee attrition, job satisfaction, salary patterns, and employee retention.

The dashboard is developed using Microsoft Power BI to transform raw HR data into meaningful business insights that help HR departments make data-driven decisions.

## Dataset Information

* Dataset: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance
* Total Records: 1,470 employees
* Data Format: CSV
* Tool: Microsoft Power BI

## Business Objectives

1. Analyze overall employee attrition.
2. Identify departments with the highest attrition.
3. Analyze employee attrition by age group and gender.
4. Understand the relationship between overtime and attrition.
5. Analyze employee satisfaction and job roles.
6. Identify factors affecting employee retention.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Employees
* Attrition Count
* Attrition Rate
* Active Employees
* Average Employee Age
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Years at Company
